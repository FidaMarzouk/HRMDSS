# HRMS User Guide

> How to use the Enova Robotics HRMS (ENOVA Pulse), screen by screen. Company rules (hours, leave entitlement,
> approvals) are in the Employee Handbook; this guide explains where to click.

## Getting started

### Signing in
- Open the HRMS and sign in with your **work email** and password. You land on **Today**.
- Your session lasts 5 hours. After that you are sent back to the login page and need to sign in again.
- First time? HR creates your account and you receive an email with a **Set my password** link (valid 24 hours).
  If it expired, use **Forgot password?** on the login page.

### Forgot or change your password
- **Forgot password:** on the login page click **Forgot password?**, enter your work email and open the link sent to
  your personal email (or work email). The link is valid for 10 minutes and works once. Choose a new password of at
  least 6 characters.
- **Change password while signed in:** open **Profile** in the sidebar, enable the password section, enter your
  current password and the new one twice, then save.

### Finding your way
- The **sidebar** groups pages under **WORK** (Today, People or My team, Leave, Time, Calendar), **INSIGHT**
  (Analytics or Team analytics, My stats) and **TALK** (Inbox, Profile). Small numbers next to a page
  show how many things wait for you there. Collapse the sidebar with the arrow button; on a phone, open it with the
  menu button.
- The **top bar** shows a greeting, today's date and week, a timeline of your shift, the bell (it counts only what
  needs your action and opens the Inbox), the theme switch (light, dark, follow your system, or by shift) and your punch
  status.
- **Search or run a command (Ctrl K):** opens the command palette. Type to find a person, a page or an action
  (Request leave, Punch in, Start break, Punch out, approve an item waiting for you, change the theme, log out), or
  ask the ENOVA Assistant. Arrow keys move, Enter runs.
- Each page has its own address (for example `/app/leave` or `/app/calendar?view=resources`), so refreshing or
  sharing a link keeps you on the same view. Old links to the former dashboards, Notifications and Chat pages are
  redirected.

## Today
- A strip of figures at the top. For reviewers: people in the building, late today, on leave or mission, and items
  awaiting you. For employees: leave days left, requests in progress and events in the next 7 days.
- **Needs your decision** (managers, HR, CEO): leave requests, event approvals and attendance corrections, soonest
  first, with warnings such as an overlap with teammates' leave or a booking clash. Click the check button to
  approve: you get a few seconds to **Undo** before it is sent. Click the arrow to open the item on its page.
  Approval is disabled for a booking that overlaps a confirmed one; open it in the Calendar to resolve it.
- **On the floor now**: each department as a row of squares showing who is in, late, on leave, on a mission or not in.
- **Your requests**: your own leave requests and corrections that are still in progress.

## Time

### Punch in and punch out
1. Open **Time** (or press Ctrl K and type "punch").
2. Click **Punch in** when you start working. The ring shows your worked time against the 8 h 30 day.
3. Use **Start break** / **End break** for lunch (between 12:30 and 14:00).
4. Click **Punch out** when you stop working (end of day, or before leaving for a site visit). You can **Punch in
   again** later the same day; each punch-in starts a new session.

Common messages:
- *"You are already punched in"*: you have an open session; punch out first.
- *"No open session found"*: you tried to punch out or take a break without being punched in.
- *"Punch-in is only allowed between 6:00 and 22:00"*: outside the allowed window.
- *"Lunch break can only be started between 12:30 and 14:00"*.

### Day ribbons
Each day is drawn as a ribbon on a 07:00–19:00 track: work in teal, breaks in grey, a late arrival in amber (after
08:30), overtime hatched (after 18:00) and a missing punch-out dashed red, with arrival, departure and hours.
- **Employees** see their own last working days.
- **Managers, HR and the CEO** see everyone in their scope for one day (move with the arrows) and can switch on
  **Show exceptions only** (late, long break, missing punch-out, repeated lateness, absence).

### Ask for a correction
1. On **Time**, under your last working days, click **Fix…** on the day.
2. Choose what happened (forgot to punch out, forgot to punch in, wrong times), give the right time(s) and a reason.
3. Click **Send request**. Your manager (or HR, or the CEO, depending on your role) decides; you are notified.

### Review corrections (managers, HR, CEO)
Corrections waiting for you are listed on **Time** and in **Needs your decision** on Today. **Approve and update**
changes the attendance record and recalculates its hours; **Reject** asks for a reason that is sent to the person.

## Leave

### Request leave
1. Open **Leave** and click **Request leave** (or Ctrl K › Request leave).
2. Pick the **start date**, **end date** and **leave type**.
3. The form shows your **remaining balance** for the year, how many **working days** (Monday–Friday) the request uses,
   and who will review it. You don't choose the reviewer; it's determined by your role and department.
4. Submit. You and the reviewer get a notification.

If the form says *"No approver assigned"*, you are not yet attached to a department manager; contact HR.
Balances restart at 0 on 1 January; leave in early January can only use what you will have earned by then.

### Follow your requests
- **My requests** lists each request's dates, type and status. Select one to see its approval steps (who has it now)
  and its history.
- *Manager Approved* still needs HR's final approval; days are only deducted after final approval.
- If a reviewer **proposed other dates**, the request shows them with **Accept new dates** and **Keep my dates**.
  Accepting moves the request to the new dates; keeping yours sends the original request back to the reviewer.
- To cancel or change a submitted request, contact your manager or HR.

### Review requests (managers, HR, CEO)
- **Pipeline** groups requests by stage: waiting on others, **Your decision**, waiting on the employee (dates
  proposed), scheduled, and closed in the last days. **All requests** is a searchable list.
- Select a request to open the decision panel: the approval steps, the balance impact, **Team coverage** (teammates ×
  working days, with days over the department's coverage limit highlighted), the **policy checks** (notice, balance,
  coverage) and, when coverage is exceeded, suggested dates.
- Decide with **Approve**, **Reject…** (with a reason) or **Propose other dates** (with an optional note).
  Keyboard: **A** approve, **R** reject, **C** propose dates, **J** / **K** next and previous request.
- After a decision you have a few seconds to **Undo** it; the requester is notified once that time has passed.
- You can't review your own requests.

## Calendar

### Views
Switch with the buttons at the top right; the arrows move by month or week, **Today** comes back to now.
- **Month**: each day shows a row of small ticks, one per event (solid = confirmed, dashed = pending, red = clash),
  the number of events and the busiest hour. Click a day to see it as a timeline, create an event on it, or open the
  week in resource lanes.
- **Week**: Monday to Sunday on a 07:00–20:00 grid.
- **List**: the month's events grouped by day.
- **Resources**: one row per room, desk, kit, robot or prototype for Monday to Friday (08:00–18:00 per day).
  Confirmed bookings are solid, pending requests dashed, clashes outlined in red; resources under maintenance are
  hatched. Turn **Show pending** off to see only confirmed bookings. Click an empty spot in a row to book that
  resource on that day.
- Filters (Month, Week and List): event type (Meeting, Mission, Booking), state (Confirmed, Pending approval, Clash,
  Declined) and department. Event types are shown by icon; colour shows the state.

### Create an event
1. Click **New event** (or click a day, or an empty spot in a resource row).
2. Choose the type: **Meeting** (description and a location, or book a room), **Mission** (description and
   destination) or **Booking** (choose what to book).
3. Set the start and end date and time (company time).
4. When a resource or room is chosen, the form checks it while you type: *Free at that time*, other **pending**
   requests at that time (you can still ask; HR approves one), or the **confirmed** booking in the way (pick another
   time or resource).
5. Choose who can see it (Everyone, My department, Only invited people) and invite people with the search list.
6. Save. Events created by employees and managers are **pending** until HR approves them; invited people are notified.

### Book a robot, room or equipment
1. Open **Calendar** › **Resources**, find the resource's row and click an empty spot on the day (or click **New event**
   and choose **Booking**).
2. Pick the resource (rooms, desks, tool kits, testing equipment, robots, prototypes), the day and the times.
3. The form tells you right away if it is free, if other requests are pending at that time, or which confirmed
   booking or maintenance is in the way.
4. Save. The booking is pending (dashed) until HR approves it, then confirmed (solid).

### Open, respond, edit or delete
Click an event to open its details: time, place, organiser, visibility, description, people invited and their answers,
and any overlaps on its resource.
- Invited? Click **Accept invitation** or **Decline invitation**; the organiser is notified.
- The organiser (or HR) can **Edit** or **Delete** it. Changing the time or resource of an approved event sends it
  back for approval.
- **HR administrators** see **Approve** and **Decline** on pending events. Approve is disabled while a confirmed
  booking overlaps the event.

## People (HR, CEO) and My team (managers)

### Find people
- Search by name, position, email or skill, and filter by department, access level or skill. Each row shows the
  person's position, access level, tenure and top skills.
- Click a person to open their profile: **Overview** (work and contact details, manager, skills with levels),
  **Time** (recent days), **Leave** (balance and requests) and, for HR, the CEO or yourself, **History** (who changed
  what and when).
- Salaries are hidden (•••• DT); HR and the CEO can reveal one, and the reveal is recorded.
- Managers (for their team) and HR can rate skills from 1 to 5; a level you set is recorded as validated by you.

### Add, edit or archive a person (HR)
1. **Add person**: name, work email (used to log in), personal email, phone, position, skills, hire date, salary,
   access (Employee or Manager) and department. An employee's manager is set from the department. Leave the password
   empty: the person receives an email to choose their own.
2. **Edit details** from the row's menu or the profile. Changing the hire date or role recalculates the leave balance.
3. **Archive…** when someone leaves: type their name to confirm and give a reason. Archived people can no longer sign
   in, but their records are kept; find them with the **Archived** filter and **Restore** them if needed. A manager
   who still leads a department or has direct reports must be replaced first.

### Departments (HR)
Switch the view to **Departments** to add or edit a department: name, manager, members and the **coverage limit**
(the most people who may be away on the same day; empty = 25% of the team, at least 1). The CEO can view them.

## Analytics (HR, CEO) and Team analytics (managers)
- Title *What needs attention this period*. Choose the period at the top right (last 30 days, this month, last month,
  this quarter, last quarter or a custom range) and whether to **compare with the previous period**. HR and the CEO
  can narrow to one department. **Export brief** downloads the figures and signals as a text file.
- **Figures**: attendance rate, punctuality, overtime and absences, each with its change against the previous period
  and one bar per working day against the target. **Why?** jumps to the chart behind it.
- **Signals**: findings ranked by urgency (coverage risk, punctuality, overtime, missing punch-outs, leave requests
  waiting, notification reads), each with the records behind it and one action.
- **Charts**: arrival swarm (arrival times per department, with the 08:30 line and the median), attendance barcode
  (one stripe per working day per person), leave tide (people away on each coming working day against the coverage
  limit) and hours budget (hours worked against contracted hours, with overtime).
- **Details**: tabs with the former dashboard charts (attendance, leave, workforce, resources and communication for
  HR; team, attendance, leave, calendar, productivity and communication for managers).
- Every chart has a **Table** button that shows the exact numbers.

## My stats
Your own figures for the chosen period, compared with the previous one: attendance rate, punctuality, production hours
and leave balance, then tabs **Overview** (monthly performance and your team), **Attendance** (your days, late
minutes, production hours and overtime), **Leave** (balance, request status, usage by type, recent requests) and
**Calendar** (upcoming events, how busy your calendar is, busiest hours, next events).

## Inbox

### Notifications
- Open **Inbox** from the sidebar or the bell. Notifications are split into **Needs action** (a request waiting for
  your decision, an invitation to answer, new dates proposed for your leave) and **For information** (everything
  else). Items leave Needs action by themselves once they are decided, by you or by someone else.
- Each notification shows the request as a card with its current status and buttons: **Approve** (with a few seconds
  to **Undo**, as on Today), **Decline**, **Accept** / **Decline** an invitation, **Accept new dates** / **Keep my
  dates**, and **Open** to go to the page. Rejecting a leave request or a correction is done from its page, where you
  can give a reason.
- **Urgent** items (something to act on today or tomorrow, sick or emergency leave) are marked as such.
- **Mark read**, **Mark all read** (For information) and the trash icon (delete) keep the list tidy. Notifications are
  deleted automatically after 30 days.
- **Daily digest at 09:00**: switch it on to receive non-urgent notifications as one summary at 09:00 on working days
  instead of one by one; urgent ones still arrive right away. Items waiting for the digest are marked "In 09:00 digest".

### Messages
- The **Messages** side of the Inbox lists your conversations with each colleague's position and whether they are in
  (with their arrival time within your department; elsewhere only in, away or out), the last message and unread counts.
- Search by name to open a conversation or start a new one.
- Type a message and press **Enter** (Shift+Enter for a new line). Messages arrive in real time; opening a
  conversation marks it read, and your last message shows "read" once the other person has seen it.
- **Share a request** (paperclip): pick one of your requests in progress (leave, events, corrections) or one of theirs
  that waits on your decision. It appears in the conversation as a card with its live status; if it waits on the
  other person, they can act on it from the card.
- Delete a message for yourself with the trash icon next to its time, or the whole conversation from the **…** menu;
  the other person keeps their copy.
- On a phone, switch between **Notifications** and **Messages** at the top of the Inbox.

## Profile
Open **Profile** from the sidebar or by clicking your initials in the top bar.

- **Access card**: your photo (click the camera to change it: JPG or PNG, up to 2 MB), name, position, department,
  today's punch state and your staff reference.
- **Balances**: annual leave left of what you have earned this year (at most 24 days a year, back to 0 on 1 January),
  and your overtime and hours worked this month.
- **Personal**: click **Edit** (or **Add**) next to a field to change only that field, then **Save**; Escape cancels.
  You can change your first and last name, phone (digits only), personal email and date of birth.
- **Work**: work email, position, department, manager, hire date and access are managed by HR and shown with a lock.
  **Message HR** opens a conversation with an HR administrator if something is wrong.
- **Skills**: add a skill with the field and **Add**; remove one with its ×. Levels (1–5) are set by your manager or HR,
  and a rated skill cannot be removed by you.
- **Preferences**: **Theme** (Light, Dark, Auto = follow your device, Shift = dark once you have punched out), saved to
  your profile so it follows you; **Daily digest at 09:00**; **Quiet hours** (switch on, choose from/to, **Save**).
- **Sign-in and security**: change your password (current password, then the new one twice, at least 6 characters);
  your other devices are signed out when it changes. **Active sessions** lists where you are signed in; **Sign out**
  ends one, **Sign out all other devices** ends all but this one, and **Log out** ends this one.
- **Log out** asks first if you are still punched in, and offers **Punch out and log out**.

The ENOVA Assistant answers from two files on the server: `Backend/knowledge/enova-robotics-handbook.md` and
`Backend/knowledge/hrms-user-guide.md`. After editing them, restart the backend or call
`POST /api/chatbot/reindex` as an administrator.

## ENOVA Assistant (chatbot)
- Click the robot button in the bottom-right corner of any page, or type a question in the command palette (Ctrl K)
  and choose *Ask the ENOVA Assistant*.
- Ask about company policies ("How is leave counted?"), how to use the HRMS ("How do I book a robot?") or your
  own data ("How many leave days do I have left?", "What meetings do I have this week?",
  "What did my attendance look like this week?").
- Answers about policies show the handbook sections they come from. The assistant can only see **your own**
  leave, attendance, calendar and profile; it cannot see other people's data or any salary.
- Click a suggestion chip to ask it directly. Use the trash icon to clear your conversation history.
- If the AI service is unavailable, the assistant shows the most relevant handbook passages instead.

## Troubleshooting
- **Sent back to the login page:** your 5-hour session expired, or you were signed out from another device or after a
  password change (the login page says so); sign in again.
- **A page is missing from your sidebar:** it belongs to another role.
- **Numbers or lists not updating live:** they refresh on their own; if not, reload the page. If it persists, contact
  HR or IT.
- **A notification is not in Needs action any more:** the request was decided or answered (maybe by someone else);
  it is under For information.
- **Leave request refused for insufficient balance:** check your balance on the Leave form; days are counted
  Monday–Friday only, and balances restart at 0 on 1 January.
- **A time record is wrong:** use **Fix…** on the Time page (up to 31 days back).
- **A booking is refused:** another confirmed booking or maintenance is in the way; the form shows which one.
- **Wrong personal information (department, position, hire date):** contact HR at hr@enovarobotics.eu.
