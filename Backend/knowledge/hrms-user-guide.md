# HRMS User Guide

> How to use the Enova Robotics HRMS, screen by screen. Company rules (hours, leave entitlement, approvals) are in
> the Employee Handbook; this guide explains where to click.

## Getting started

### Signing in
- Open the HRMS and sign in with your **work email** and password. You are taken to the dashboard for your role
  (Employee, Manager, HR Administrator or CEO).
- Your session lasts 5 hours. After that you are sent back to the login page and need to sign in again.
- First time? HR creates your account and you receive an email with a **Set my password** link (valid 24 hours).
  If it expired, use **Forgot password?** on the login page.

### Forgot or change your password
- **Forgot password:** on the login page click **Forgot password?**, enter your work email and open the link sent to
  your personal email (or work email). The link is valid for 10 minutes and works once. Choose a new password of at
  least 6 characters.
- **Change password while signed in:** open **Profile** (bottom of the sidebar), enable the password section, enter
  your current password and the new one twice, then save.

### Navigating the dashboard
- The **sidebar** on the left lists the pages available to your role. Collapse or expand it with the arrow button.
  On a phone, open it with the menu button (top-left) and use the quick navigation bar under the header.
- At the bottom of the sidebar: **Chat**, **Notifications**, **Profile** and **Logout**.
- The header shows a greeting, today's date, the notification bell (with the number of unread notifications), quick
  buttons for Calendar and Attendance, and your initials (click them to open your Profile).
- The current page is kept in the address bar (for example `?page=leave`), so refreshing the browser keeps you on
  the same page.

## Attendance page

### Punch in and punch out
1. Open **Attendance** in the sidebar (or the activity button in the header).
2. Click **Punch In** when you start working. The status changes to *Checked In*.
3. Click **Punch Out** when you stop working (end of day, or before leaving for a site visit).
4. You can punch in again later the same day; each punch-in starts a new session. The **Today's Activity Timeline**
   shows all of today's sessions.

Common messages:
- *"You are already punched in"*: you have an open session; punch out first.
- *"No open session found"*: you tried to punch out or take a break without being punched in.
- *"Punch-in is only allowed between 6:00 and 22:00"*: outside the allowed window.

### Lunch break
- While punched in, click **Start Break** between 12:30 and 14:00, and **End Break** when you are back.
- The break duration is subtracted from your production hours. Punching out during a break ends the break.

### Attendance reports
Below the punch buttons, the reports table lists attendance records for a date range (default: last 30 days).
Change the start and end dates and refresh. Employees see their own records, managers see their team (including
themselves), and HR and the CEO see everyone. Columns show date, check-in, check-out, status (Present / Late /
Absent), break time, overtime and production hours.

## Leave Requests page

### Request leave
1. Open **Leave Requests** and click **Request Leave**.
2. Pick the **start date** and **end date** and the **leave type**.
3. The form shows your **remaining balance** and how many **working days** (Monday–Friday) the request uses, and who
   will review it (**Will be reviewed by**). You don't choose the reviewer; it's determined by your role and
   department.
4. Click **Submit request**. You and the reviewer get a notification.

If the form says *"No approver assigned"*, you are not yet attached to a department manager; contact HR.

### Follow your requests
- The table shows each request's dates, type, reviewer and **status** (Pending, Manager Approved, Admin Approved,
  CEO Approved, or a Rejected status). Click **View** for details.
- Use the search box, the **status** and **type** filters and the sort order to find a request.
- *Manager Approved* still needs HR's final approval; days are only deducted after final approval.
- To cancel or change a submitted request, contact your manager or HR.

### Review requests (managers, HR, CEO)
- **Managers:** the **Team requests** tab lists requests from your team. Pending requests show **Approve** and
  **Reject** buttons. After you approve, the request goes to HR for final approval.
- **HR administrators:** the **Awaiting HR decision** tab lists employee and manager requests that are Pending or
  Manager Approved. Approving gives final approval and updates the employee's balance.
- **CEO:** the **HR administrators' requests** tab lists leave requested by HR administrators.
- You can't review your own requests. A confirmation dialog shows the request details before you decide, and the
  requester is notified of the decision.

## Calendar page

### Views and filters
- Switch between **month**, **week**, **day** and **list** views and move with the arrows.
- Click **Filter** to show only some event types (meeting, mission, resource reservation), statuses (pending,
  approved, declined) or departments.
- Click an event to see its details, participants and their responses.

### Create an event
1. Click **Create Event**.
2. Choose the type:
   - **Meeting**: title, description, location, start and end, participants.
   - **Mission**: title, description, destination, start and end, participants.
   - **Resource reservation**: choose the resource type and the resource; availability and conflicts are shown
     before you save.
3. Choose the **visibility**: Public (everyone), Department (your department) or Private (you and participants).
4. Save. Events created by employees and managers are **pending** until HR approves them; participants are notified.

### Book a robot, room or equipment
Create an event of type **Resource reservation**, choose the type (desktop, meeting room, office, robot, tool kit,
testing equipment, prototype) and the resource. Resources already booked for overlapping times or under maintenance
can't be booked; the form tells you which reservation conflicts.

### Respond, edit or delete
- Invited? Open the event and click **Accept** or **Decline**; the organizer is notified.
- The organizer (or HR) can **Edit Event** or **Delete** it. Changing the time or resource of an approved event
  sends it back for approval.
- **HR administrators** see **Approve** / **Decline** buttons on pending events.

## Team Chat
- Open **Chat** from the sidebar. The left panel lists colleagues and your conversations with their unread counts;
  search by name.
- Select a colleague to open the conversation, type a message and press **Enter** (Shift+Enter for a new line).
- Messages arrive in real time. Opening a conversation marks its messages as read.
- Open a message's options menu to delete it (it disappears for you only). **Delete conversation** clears the conversation from
  your view; the other person keeps their copy.

## Notifications
- The bell in the header shows how many notifications are unread. New ones also pop up briefly in the corner.
- The **Notifications** page lists them with filters. Mark one or all as read, or delete them.
- You are notified about leave requests and decisions, event invitations, approvals and responses.
- Notifications are deleted automatically after 30 days.

## Profile
- Open **Profile** from the sidebar or by clicking your initials in the header.
- You can update your first and last name, phone number, birthdate, skills and profile picture (JPG or PNG,
  up to 2 MB), and change your password (current password required).
- Your work email, position, department, salary and hire date are managed by HR and shown read-only. Ask HR to
  correct them.

## Dashboards

### Personal dashboard (all roles)
Your own indicators for the selected date range:
- **Attendance Rate**: days present or late ÷ expected working days (approved leave excluded).
- **Punctuality Score**: share of your attendance days without a late arrival.
- **Production Hours** and **Production Hours Trend**: worked hours minus breaks.
- **Overtime Hours**: minutes before 08:00 and after 18:00.
- **Leave Balance**, **Leave Request Status** and **Leave Usage By Type**.
- **Upcoming Events**, **Events by Type** and **Calendar Density** (how busy your calendar is).
- **Team Members** and your **Manager**.
- A monthly performance score combining attendance (30%), punctuality (20%), productivity (40%) and event
  participation (10%), compared with the same months last year.

### Team dashboard (managers)
- **Team Attendance Rate**, **Absenteeism Rate**, **Late Arrivals Trend**, **Avg Check-In Time**.
- **Pending Leave Requests**, **Leave Approval Statistics**, **Department Leave Calendar** (who is away when),
  **Leave Distribution by Type**.
- **Avg Production Hours**, **Overtime Trends**, **Team Productivity Breakdown**, **Most Active Team Members**.
- **Team Skills Distribution**, **Team Tenure**, **Unread Messages** and **Avg Response Time** to team messages.

### HR dashboard (HR administrators and CEO)
- Headcount: **Total Employees**, **Employees by Department**, **Headcount & Growth**, **Recent Hires**,
  **Manager-Employee Ratio**.
- Attendance: **Attendance by Department**, **Absenteeism Trend**, **Late Arrivals by Department**,
  **Overtime by Department**.
- Leave: **Leave Status**, **Leave Days Usage**, **Leave Types**, **Leave Seasonal Patterns**.
- Compensation: **Salary Distribution** (HR and CEO only).
- Resources: **Resource Utilization**, **Maintenance by Resource Type**, **Most Requested Resources**.
- Communication: **Peak Communication Times**, **Messages by Department**, **Notification Read Rates**,
  **Response Time Distribution**.
- Use the date range picker at the top to change the period.

## Administration (HR administrators)

### Add or edit an employee
1. Open **Employees** › **Employees** tab › **Add Employee**.
2. Fill in name, work email (used to log in), personal email, phone, position, skills, hire date, salary, role
   (Employee or Manager) and department. An employee's manager is set automatically from the department.
3. Leave the password empty: the employee receives an email with a link to choose their own password. No
   password is ever sent by email.
4. To edit, click **Edit** on the row. Changing the hire date recalculates the leave balance. Setting a new password
   notifies the employee that it changed (without including it).
5. **Delete** removes the employee after confirmation.

### Manage departments
Open **Employees** › **Departments** tab. **Add Department** lets you set the name, the manager and the members;
**Edit** changes them (members moved from another department are removed from it); **Delete** removes the
department and unassigns its members.

### Update the assistant's knowledge
The ENOVA Assistant answers from two files on the server: `Backend/knowledge/enova-robotics-handbook.md` and
`Backend/knowledge/hrms-user-guide.md`. After editing them, restart the backend or call
`POST /api/chatbot/reindex` as an administrator.

## ENOVA Assistant (chatbot)
- Click the robot button in the bottom-right corner of any page.
- Ask about company policies ("How is leave counted?"), how to use the HRMS ("How do I book a robot?") or your
  own data ("How many leave days do I have left?", "What meetings do I have this week?",
  "What did my attendance look like this week?").
- Answers about policies show the handbook sections they come from. The assistant can only see **your own**
  leave, attendance, calendar and profile; it cannot see other people's data or any salary.
- Click a suggestion chip to ask it directly. Use the trash icon to clear your conversation history.
- If the AI service is unavailable, the assistant shows the most relevant handbook passages instead.

## Troubleshooting
- **Sent back to the login page:** your 5-hour session expired; sign in again.
- **"Access denied" page:** the page belongs to another role; use **Back to my dashboard**.
- **Notifications or chat not updating live:** refresh the page; if it persists, contact HR or IT.
- **Leave request refused for insufficient balance:** check your balance on the Leave Requests form; days are
  counted Monday–Friday only.
- **Wrong personal information (department, position, hire date):** contact HR at hr@enovarobotics.eu.
